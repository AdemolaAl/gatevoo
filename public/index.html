FAQ
Questions? Straight answers.

    

      
Does Gatevoo hold my money?+
No. Customers pay straight into your own wallet. Gatevoo only watches your public address to see when a payment lands, so it can never spend, hold or freeze your funds.

      
Do I need a registered company?+
No. You add your wallet address and start. Because Gatevoo never holds money, there is no business verification step to get going.

      
Which coins can customers pay with?+
USDT on TRON (TRC20) and Bitcoin today. USDC, Ethereum, Solana and cards are on the way.

      
How fast is a payment confirmed?+
USDT usually confirms in about a minute. Bitcoin confirms after one block, which averages about 10 minutes. The checkout shows the customer each step as it happens.

      
What if a customer sends the wrong amount?+
Overpayments are matched and flagged so you can refund the extra. Short or unclear payments go to a review list in your dashboard, where you match them in one tap. Nothing is lost.

      
Will it work on my website?+
Yes. Add a Pay with crypto button that opens the checkout as a pop-up, or send customers to a payment link. Your site is told the moment a payment confirms so it can unlock the order.

      
When can I use it?+
Gatevoo is being built into Zedapex's own apps first. Join the waitlist and we'll invite you as spots open.
